    Cloud cameras (Qubo and others)¶

    Some cameras only talk to their maker's cloud: they have no RTSP stream and no ONVIF, so Minder can't watch their video directly. Qubo's are like this. Minder can still use them, through Home Assistant and a community integration that speaks the maker's cloud. That support is an add-on: off until you turn it on, and removable without a trace.

    A cloud camera works with rules, alerts, history, search and face recognition like any other camera. What it can't do, and why, is at the end of this page.

    How a cloud camera's alert works¶

    1. The camera's cloud reports motion. Qubo pushes the event as it happens.
    2. Minder looks before deciding. It waits for the camera's picture of that moment (Qubo's arrives 5–10 s later), takes up to four stills a second apart, and runs its own detector on them, with the camera's ignore areas applied. A picture taken before the motion is never judged.
    3. Rules match what was in the pictures:
    4. Confirmed: one event per thing seen (a person, a car, a dog), so a "car on the driveway" rule works on a cloud camera, and a cat no longer arrives as a person.
    5. Unconfirmed: a fresh picture with nothing in it. You still get the alert, marked "unconfirmed, nothing visible in the camera's pictures", because a few stills taken seconds late can miss someone. To skip these for the whole box, set MINDER_HA_UNCONFIRMED=drop. A single rule can skip them with require_confirmed: true; the Rules tab doesn't offer this yet.
    6. Unchecked: no picture arrived in time. The alert goes out as a plain motion alert, because a missing picture proves nothing either way.
    7. The alert carries the picture it was found on, with the detection boxed.

    The same check applies to every camera connected through Home Assistant, not only Qubo.

    Turning on the Qubo add-on¶

    1. Settings → Add-ons → Qubo cloud cameras → Enable.
    2. Read the risks it shows first: it's unofficial, it depends on Qubo's cloud, and Home Assistant keeps your Qubo password on the box so it can sign in again.
    3. Enabling installs a reviewed, pinned version of the integration into Home Assistant, which restarts. Devices may not respond for about a minute.
    4. Cameras → Add a cloud camera. Sign in with your Qubo email and password.
    5. Use an email-based Qubo account. Phone (OTP) sign-ins expire after an hour. If yours is a phone account, create an email account and share your cameras to it from the Qubo app.
    6. The password goes straight to Home Assistant; Minder doesn't store or log it.
    7. Pick your cameras. Minder lists the account's devices and what each can give it:
    8. Add when the device sends motion events;
    9. Can't add when it doesn't, because without motion there is nothing to start an alert.
    10. Add a rule for the new camera in the Rules tab, as for any camera.

    Added cameras appear on the Cameras tab with a ☁ cloud badge and their latest picture.

    Tested so far: the Qubo Video Doorbell Pro (HCD04C). Qubo's indoor and outdoor cameras may work; the add-on lists what has been tested.

    New and removed devices on your account¶

    • A new camera on your Qubo account appears under Add a cloud camera once Home Assistant has loaded it, which usually means after Home Assistant restarts. Minder never adds a camera on its own: a device someone shares to your account shouldn't start alerting by itself.
    • A camera taken off your account stops sending events. Remove it from the Cameras tab (remove on its card).

    Turning it off¶

    Settings → Add-ons → Disable removes, in order: 1. the cloud cameras it added. A rule that watched only them is switched off, never widened to other cameras; 2. the account's sign-in in Home Assistant; 3. the integration itself.

    Home Assistant restarts once.

    What a cloud camera can't do¶

    Why
    Live view Qubo's cloud lets one viewer watch at a time
    Alerts in under a few seconds Minder waits for the camera's picture (5–10 s for Qubo)
    Work without the internet events and pictures come from the maker's servers
    Detection between motion events Minder only sees what the cloud reports
    Keep everything in the house the maker's cloud sees the camera's events and pictures; see Privacy

    Other brands: Minder's add-ons are a reviewed library. A brand whose Home Assistant integration exposes a motion sensor and a picture can be added as a new add-on. Recorded video on a network drive (as Xiaomi cameras can write) is being looked at as a fully local alternative.
