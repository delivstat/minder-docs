    Searching what the cameras saw¶

    Ask for events by how something looked, not only by when or where it happened:

    "when did someone carry a bag to the door this week" "show me the light green car yesterday" "was there a delivery person this morning" "when did a person in a red dress come by"

    Minder answers with the camera events that look most like the description, best first, each with its time, camera and photo. It works in chat and in the web portal.

    Search is optional and off by default. It runs entirely on the appliance: no picture or description leaves your network.

    What it is good at, and what it is not¶

    Search ranks. Given a description, it puts the events that look most like it first. It does that well for things a camera can plainly see: colours, clothing, vehicles, animals, something carried, a child or an adult.

    It does not decide whether something happened. Every answer is worded "closest matches, not confirmed sightings", and it never answers "yes". Before search was built, the model was measured on a real installation's own events. It was good at putting the right pictures on top, and unreliable at a yes/no line: told to say yes above a fixed score, it was right about half the time. So Minder shows you the closest few and lets you look.

    What this means in practice:

    • Look at the photos. The first match is the most similar event, not proof of one. If nothing in the window looks like your description, you still get the three least unlike it.
    • Search does not raise alerts, and can't be made into an alert rule. Alerts stay with detection, rules and face recognition.
    • It describes, it does not identify. "A man in a blue shirt" finds men in blue shirts. Whether one of them was someone you know is face recognition's job (see Face detection and recognition).
    • Small or distant subjects are harder. Search looks at the detected object's own crop from the camera's detection stream. A person far from the camera is a few dozen pixels tall, and fine details like a logo or a lanyard won't be found.

    Turning it on¶

    ./minder search enable

    This downloads the search model (about 210 MB, checked against pinned checksums; nothing is kept if a check fails), switches search on and restarts Minder. It then works through the last seven days of camera events in the background, 150 at a time every five minutes. A week is typically 1,000 to 2,000 events, so expect it to take up to about an hour and a quarter; ./minder search status shows the count growing, and search already works on what is indexed. After that, each new event is added a few minutes after it ends.

    ./minder search status     # on? installed? how many events are indexed?
./minder search disable    # switch off, delete the model AND everything indexed

    Indexing runs alongside everything else and never sits on the alert path. If the appliance is busy, indexing falls behind; alerts don't. It costs a couple of minutes of CPU a day on a typical house.

    Asking in chat¶

    Ask a history question the way you normally would, and describe what you're looking for. Say when, or ask "when did…": that's what tells Minder you mean the past. Without either, "a person in a red dress" asks the cameras what they see now, and you get a live answer instead of a search.

    "did anyone wearing a helmet come to the gate today" "show me the white van this week" "a brown dog in the garden yesterday"

    Minder searches when the question describes how something looked. A question about only when, where or who gets the ordinary history answer it always did:

    You ask Minder does
    "what happened at the main door this afternoon" history: everything that happened, with photos
    "who was at the door last night" history: who was seen, recognised faces named
    "someone in a yellow jacket at the main door this afternoon" search: the closest matches

    The time, camera and kind of thing you mention still narrow the search. "a person in a red dress at the gate yesterday" ranks only yesterday's people at the gate. Ask "when did…" without naming a period ("when did a person in a red dress come by") and search covers everything indexed (the last seven days).

    A typical answer:

    🔎 Closest to “someone carry a bag to the door” among 412 camera event(s) in that time:
• Tue 14:02 Front Door — person
• Mon 09:47 Front Door — person
• Mon 18:15 Porch — person

These are the closest matches, not confirmed sightings.

    with the three photos, each with a box around the thing that matched.

    Searching in the portal¶

    Dashboard → Events → History. Set the period, camera and What as usual, and type a description into Looks like. Instead of the history list, you get a grid of the closest matches, best first. Choose Any time to search everything indexed. Leave Looks like empty and the form works exactly as before.

    Privacy¶

    Search stores, for each camera event, a list of numbers summarising what the detected object looked like. These numbers aren't a picture and can't be turned back into one. They don't identify anyone, and they mean nothing without the camera photos they came from. They're still derived from images of your home, so:

    • they stay on the appliance, in the same local database as the event history;
    • they're never sent anywhere, and the search model runs locally;
    • ./minder search disable deletes them all, along with the model.

    Photos are Frigate's: kept for seven days by default. An event older than that can still be found by search, but may no longer have a picture.

    Settings¶

    All optional, in .env:

    Variable Default What it does
    MINDER_SEARCH off set by ./minder search enable / disable
    MINDER_SEARCH_INDEX_INTERVAL 300 seconds between indexing passes
    MINDER_SEARCH_PER_CYCLE 150 most events indexed in one pass, so a backfill spreads out
    MINDER_SEARCH_TOP 3 matches shown in a chat answer
    MINDER_SEARCH_TEXT_IDLE_S 600 the text half of the model is unloaded after this long unused
    MINDER_SEARCH_THREADS 2 CPU threads the model may use
    MINDER_SEARCH_MODEL_DIR /data/search where the model lives

    Troubleshooting¶

    "Search is off" in the portal, or chat gives ordinary history answers to descriptive questions: run ./minder search status. Search needs both enabled and installed.

    "Nothing is indexed for that yet": the backfill is still running, or the window has no events. ./minder search status shows the count growing.

    A match is obviously wrong: expected now and then (see What it is good at). Rephrase with what the camera can see: "a person in an orange shirt" works better than "the plumber".
